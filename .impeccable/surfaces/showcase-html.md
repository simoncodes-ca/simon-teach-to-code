# Showcase surface

## Scope & mode

Root `showcase.html`; Experience surface for a child and parent exploring a coding journey.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

A beginner and parent need to understand what is ready, what comes next, and where a project leads. The primary action is opening an available project. Proof is the real project sequence and teaching topics in `ROADMAP.md`. Must remain one self-contained static file, runnable from the filesystem, accessible, and honest about future work.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A storybook trail map makes the learning progression memorable; it refuses the conventional flat dashboard grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** A midnight ink field with warm parchment trail pieces, painted terrain, stamped route markers, and a single coral signal for available work. Cards feel like illustrated map landmarks, not generic UI tiles.

**STORY:** The visitor follows a winding route from Calculator and Elevator into the future strategy-game horizon, understanding each stop as a skill milestone and choosing a live stop to enter.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact map legend and title sit above an illustrated route that immediately connects the two live project landmarks. The route continues below into future landmarks. The first live card is the clearest action and links directly to its HTML file.

**FORM:** Winding trail map with irregularly placed landmark cards and connective linework; tuned for scanning on desktop, then becomes a readable vertical trail on mobile. Signature interaction is landmark hover/focus: the card lifts, the route marker brightens, and its teaching topics become more legible.

**RISK:** An illustrated map can become decorative noise. Keep labels, sequence numbers, state labels, and real links explicit so the metaphor improves wayfinding rather than obscuring it.

## Unresolved decisions

Exact illustration palette and whether to include decorative landscape vignettes are implementation decisions; no external assets are required.
